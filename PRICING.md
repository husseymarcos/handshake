# Handshake Pricing Strategy

## Current Model: Ultra-Low Entry (Iterative Approach)

**Status:** Pre-launch  
**Target:** Students and junior professionals  
**Value Metric:** Resume adaptations (generated PDFs)  
**Model:** Pay-per-use credit packs

---

## Pricing Tiers

| Pack | Credits | Price | Per-Credit | Best For |
|------|---------|-------|------------|----------|
| **Free** | 5 | $0 | $0 | First-time users, testing the product |
| **Starter** | 15 | $5 | $0.33 | Casual job seekers, early search |
| **Job Seeker** ⭐ | 40 | $10 | $0.25 | Active searchers (~2 months of applying) |

**Recommended tier:** Job Seeker (highlighted as "Best Value")

---

## Credit Rules

- **No expiration** — Credits never expire
- **Top-ups available** — Users can buy additional packs anytime
- **Minimum purchase** — $5 (Starter pack)
- **No subscriptions** — One-time purchases only

---

## Value Proposition

### For Students/Juniors

> "One tailored resume = one shot at your dream job"

**Student math:**
- $0.25 per resume vs. 2+ hours manual editing
- $10 for their entire job search
- Cheaper than a career counseling session

### Competitive Positioning

| Alternative | Cost | Time | Quality |
|-------------|------|------|---------|
| Manual editing | Free | 2+ hours/resume | Variable |
| ChatGPT prompting | Free-$20/mo | 30-60 min | Inconsistent formatting |
| Professional resume writer | $100-300 | 1 week turnaround | High (one-time) |
| **Handshake** | **$0.25/resume** | **5 minutes** | **Consistently perfect** |

---

## Trust Builders (No Job Guarantee)

1. **7-Day Refund** — Unused credits refunded within 7 days, no questions asked
2. **Free Tier** — 5 full adaptations to experience the value before paying
3. **Quality Preview** — Side-by-side comparison of manual vs. Handshake resume
4. **Social Proof** — "Join X job seekers who landed interviews"

---

## Pricing Psychology

- **Price anchoring:** Show per-credit cost ($0.33 → $0.25) to make Job Seeker pack feel like a deal
- **Impulse-friendly:** $5 is less than a latte; $10 is less than a textbook
- **No guilt:** Credits don't expire, so no "wasted subscription" feeling
- **Outcome-focused:** One credit = one job application = one potential job offer

---

## Conversion Flow

1. **Sign up** → 5 free credits instantly
2. **Use 2-3 credits** → Soft prompt: "Running low? Get 15 more for $5"
3. **Use all 5 credits** → Hard prompt: "Upgrade to keep applying"
4. **Purchase** → Simple Stripe checkout, credits added immediately
5. **Post-purchase** → Confirmation + encouragement to apply to more jobs

## Notes

**Why not monthly subscription?**
Job search is temporary. A subscription creates "waste guilt" when they're not actively applying. Pay-per-use aligns price with actual usage and feels fairer.

**Why so cheap?**
We're optimizing for:
1. **Volume** — Get feedback and testimonials fast
2. **Low friction** — Students are price-sensitive
3. **Iteration** — Validate the model, then raise prices based on data

**When to raise prices:**
- Conversion rate > 10% at current prices
- Strong testimonials: "Handshake got me 5 interviews"
- Word-of-mouth growth without paid marketing
